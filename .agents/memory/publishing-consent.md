---
name: Publishing consent boundaries
description: Why account-wide automation is not blanket permission for all historical or TikTok content
---

Automatic publishing starts with clips completed after an account is enabled; it does not silently backfill the historical vault.

**Why:** Enabling future automation should not unexpectedly publish previously stored private material.

**How to apply:** Preserve this boundary when changing account preferences, allocation, reconnect behavior or scheduling.

TikTok account-wide opt-in does not replace the per-video preview, creator-provided privacy choices and music-usage consent.

**Why:** TikTok's Content Sharing Guidelines impose content-specific UX requirements. A “fully automatic” label must not remove these provider requirements.

**How to apply:** Preserve a review gate for TikTok even when other approved providers can proceed automatically. Do not preselect privacy or imply that app approval proves content rights.
